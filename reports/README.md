# Round reports 战报

Round-by-round reports from the production league this framework was
extracted from: China Sports Lottery football fixtures, analysed daily,
locked before kickoff, settled against results, and reviewed in full —
**losing rounds included, nothing deleted, nothing edited after the fact.**

How to read a report:

- **Pushed calls** are the league's public positions: confidence medium or
  above after all downgrades. Everything else is recorded but not pushed.
- **No-bet audit**: every no-bet is settled against the call the analyst
  declared they *would* have made. Avoiding a loser counts as correct;
  avoiding a winner is logged as a cost, not hidden.
- **Cumulative snapshot**: the audited totals after that round, so any
  single round can be checked against the long-run record.
